Local Video — Tactical Overlay Player

Select a video already stored on your device. It plays entirely in the browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
The overlay canvas is synced to video.currentTime, so tactical graphics stay locked to playback position — including on seek, pause, and scrub. Player positions shown here are simulated (deterministic function of playback time) as a stand-in for a real tracking feed; swap getOverlayState() for a lookup into your actual per-frame tracking data to drive this from a real match.
